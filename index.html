The studio

We make the game we want on our own phones.

No Work Play is a small independent team. We started because the games we liked playing had turned into part-time jobs — login bonuses, decaying streaks, timed events. So we built the opposite: calm, generous, finished-feeling games you can enjoy in a queue.

Small and slow

We ship fewer games and support them longer. When something is released, it keeps getting fixes instead of being abandoned for the next launch.

Kind by design

Difficult levels offer help before they offer a paywall. If a player is stuck, that is our problem to solve, not theirs to pay for.

Quietly honest

No fake countdowns, no invented discounts, no "limited time" offers that return every week. What you see is what it costs.

Flagship

NoNoWork — a calm puzzle to fill a quiet minute.

Our flagship title is a gentle logic puzzle with no clock and no fail state. Every board can be finished at your own speed, hints are free and unlimited, and progress saves the instant you touch a piece.

It plays completely offline, has no energy system, and never sends a notification asking you to come back. Leave it for a month — it will be exactly where you left it.

Our promise

Four things you will never see in our games.

01

No energy bars

You will never be told to wait, or to pay, before you are allowed to keep playing.

02

No streaks

Nothing decays while you are away. Missing a day costs you nothing at all.

03

No forced ads

Every ad is optional and clearly marked. Watching one is a choice you make for a bonus.

04

No data selling

We do not sell personal information. Our privacy policy says exactly what we collect, in plain words.
